Supply Chain Copilot — Microsoft Fabric

Microsoft FabricOneLakePySparkLLMPower BI

Business Problem

Logistics operations lacked a systematic way to diagnose shipment delays. Root-cause analysis was manual, slow, and inconsistent — preventing operations teams from acting on delay patterns before they escalated into SLA breaches.

Solution

Designed a Medallion Lakehouse on Microsoft Fabric ingesting logistics data across Bronze (raw), Silver (validated), and Gold (analytical) layers — with PySpark transformation logic and an LLM layer that analyses Gold-layer delay records to identify and explain root causes, surfaced in Power BI for operations leadership.

Architecture & Stack

Microsoft FabricOneLakeDataflowsPySparkOpen-Source LLMPower BIMedallion Architecture
  • Bronze layer: raw logistics event ingestion via Fabric Dataflows
  • Silver layer: PySpark validation, deduplication, schema enforcement
  • Gold layer: aggregated shipment KPIs, delay flags, carrier performance
  • LLM analysis layer: pattern-based delay root-cause generation from Gold data
  • Power BI semantic model connected to Gold layer for real-time delay intelligence

What This Proves

End-to-end Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse implementation combined with applied LLM reasoning — demonstrating ability to build modern, AI-augmented supply chain intelligence platforms on the Microsoft data stack.

Time-Series · Statistical Forecasting

Multi-Model Petrol Price Forecasting Engine

SARIMAARIMAHolt-WintersPythonPandas

Business Problem

Procurement teams needed a reliable forward cost view for petrol prices to inform purchase planning and budgeting decisions — currently relying on lagging spot-price data with no predictive outlook.

Solution

Benchmarked three statistical time-series forecasting models on 12 years of national petrol price data, selecting the best-performing model for production use — delivering a 4–6 week cost outlook that procurement teams could use directly for planning.

Methodology

  • 12-year national petrol price dataset collection and preprocessing
  • Stationarity testing (ADF), seasonal decomposition, ACF/PACF analysis
  • SARIMA: seasonal parameter tuning (p,d,q)(P,D,Q)m grid search
  • ARIMA: non-seasonal baseline with AIC/BIC optimisation
  • Holt-Winters: triple exponential smoothing with additive/multiplicative seasonality
  • Train/test split evaluation: MAPE, RMSE, MAE across all models
  • SARIMA selected: 3.17% training MAPE, 15.65% testing MAPE

Impact

3.17%
Training MAPE (SARIMA)
4–6 wk
Forward cost view

What This Proves

Deep time-series modelling capability — the ability to select, tune, and rigorously evaluate statistical forecasting models against a real business planning need, not just run a tutorial notebook.
